Our story

Your companion
at the table.

Like everyone else, the team at PlatePal was trying to reach our weight and body goals—some of us wanted to build muscle, while others focused on weight loss. But we all encountered the same problem: we were getting fed up with the high cost of calorie tracking apps (upwards of $200/year) and/or the ad-infested “free” tiers that were unusable. Keeping track of our meals felt harder than it needed to be, so we launched PlatePal: your companion at the table.

Meet PlatePal

More than a calorie tracker.

PlatePal is an early-stage AI startup focused on the idea of having a “companion” that follows and tracks your meals with you. The base plan contains all the essentials with computer vision: identifying foods and their respective calorie counts. But we've also implemented more machine learning technology to bring life to PlatePal, which can now act as your companion by suggesting additions to your plate, updating daily and weekly calorie goals, and tracking other nutritional benchmarks to help you reach your body goals, whatever they may be.
